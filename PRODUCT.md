# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

ML systems reviewers, inference engineers, research collaborators, and technically curious portfolio readers who need a standalone explanation of the Lumo FlyWheel GDN tree-scan work without reading earlier volumes first.

## Product Purpose

This GitHub Pages surface publishes an expressive research paper for lossless GDN tree verification. It should answer what was built, how it differs from native MTP and prior tree decoding, why the design choices were necessary, and what the evidence says about speed and losslessness.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, inspectable, and candid. The page should feel like a systems paper with source receipts, not a glossy launch page or a benchmark advertisement.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-lab marketing, vague glossy claims, ungrounded hero metrics, unexplained internal jargon, and paper-preview language that says the real proof will come later.

## Design Principles

- Make the verifier contract the protagonist: tree-aware attention plus branch-local GDN state.
- Keep claims tied to source artifacts, measurements, and explicit caveats.
- Explain specialized terms in the flow of the paper so a fresh CS graduate can follow without Vol. I or II.
- Show the discovery trail, including failed designs and measurement corrections, because that is part of the contribution.
- Preserve the dark, technical reading environment while keeping mobile and desktop layouts easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dark mode is the default. Text contrast, keyboard-focus states, reduced-motion handling, responsive layout, and scrollable wide tables should remain intact.
