- speed_thesis: Use vLLM ngram speculative decoding with a larger speculative depth than the prior best while keeping the lookup window narrow. The 3-token, min-2, max-8 surface showed the only material decode lift so far; moving to 5 speculative tokens can increase accepted-token bursts on repeated prompt/output spans without changing model weights or sampling behavior.
- expected_affected_counter: Warm decode tok/s in the real vLLM workload window. The candidate primarily targets the speculative accept path and per-token scheduler overhead, not the underlying FP8 GEMM kernels.
- quality_risk: Moderate. Ngram speculative decoding should be verifier-bounded, but prior 3-token min-2 max-8 failed B-1 with empty or truncated concurrent outputs. This candidate keeps prompt_lookup_max at 8 instead of broadening the search window, so it avoids the aggressive max-16/max-32 shapes that failed measurement while still changing speculative depth.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not a CUTLASS tile, schedule, stage, or caller mutation, so it avoids the exhausted FP8 GEMM negative-memory path. It is also not a request_shaping-only or flat vllm_config capacity candidate. It does not repeat the exhausted 2-token ngram family or the 3-token min-2 max-only family; the serving surface changes to a new speculative-depth/minimum pair.
