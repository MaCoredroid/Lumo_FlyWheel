Secondary counters may show reduced DeltaNet kernel time or fewer launch stalls if the controller captures server-side timing.
- quality_risk: Low to moderate. The candidate does not alter sampling, weights, tokenizer behavior, or speculative acceptance, but it does route a model-specific kernel through a different Triton implementation. Any numerical drift, boundary-condition issue, or unsupported shape should be caught by B-1 before speed ranking and by the real-workload measurement if the launch path is incompatible.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not another CUTLASS schedule/tile/stage mutation, so it does not repeat the prior bandwidth-bounded FP8 GEMM negative memory. It is also not a request-shaping or max_num_seqs/max_num_batched_tokens/gpu_memory_utilization candidate, which controller guidance says are exhausted or flat. Unlike the faster ngram speculative candidates, it cannot truncate or empty concurrent outputs through speculative decode acceptance because no speculative decoding surface is enabled.
