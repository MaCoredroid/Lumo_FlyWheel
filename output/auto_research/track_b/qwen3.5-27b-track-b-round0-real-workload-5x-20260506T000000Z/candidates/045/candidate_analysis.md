- speed_thesis: Use vLLM ngram speculative decoding with a stricter prompt-lookup window (`prompt_lookup_min=6`, `prompt_lookup_max=8`) and moderate depth (`num_speculative_tokens=4`). The thesis is that repeated warm-workload prompt/output scaffolding can still yield accepted speculative tokens, reducing target-model decode steps, while the higher minimum lookup length avoids speculative matches from short ambiguous fragments.
- expected_affected_counter: Primary effect should be visible in decode-time warm TPS from `throughput.json`. Secondary diagnostic counters should include speculative acceptance rate, target decode steps per generated token, and B-1 empty/truncated-output incidence.
- quality_risk: Moderate. Ngram speculation can still stress concurrent output assembly, but this surface reduces the main observed risk by requiring a longer prompt match before proposing tokens and by capping lookup span at 8. If no reliable 6-token prompt match exists, vLLM should fall back toward ordinary target-model decoding rather than forcing low-confidence proposals.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not another CUTLASS tile/schedule/stage mutation, not request shaping, and not a flat launch-capacity-only vLLM knob. It is distinct from the exhausted 2-token family, the exhausted 3-token/min-2 family, the failed 3-token/min-3/max-6 runtime shape, the baseline-level 3-token/min-4/max-8 and 5-token/min-4/max-8 shapes, and the pending 4-token/min-5/max-8 surface. The higher minimum lookup length directly targets the prior speculative-decode B-1 failures with empty or truncated concurrent outputs.
