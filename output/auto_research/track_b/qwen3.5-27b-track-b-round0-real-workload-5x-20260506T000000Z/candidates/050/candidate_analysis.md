- expected_affected_counter: Primary counter is warm decode-time TPS in `throughput.json`; secondary diagnostic movement should appear as lower per-token decode latency without relying on aggregate concurrent wall-clock throughput. If successful, the improvement should come from attention backend time rather than batching, max sequence capacity, or prefix-cache effects.
- quality_risk: Low. This keeps target weights, sampling behavior, request shape, and output constraints unchanged. The main risk is runtime backend incompatibility or a fallback to default attention; numeric drift should be limited to normal alternate-kernel floating point differences and should be far lower risk than speculative decoding truncation or guided-decoding constraint changes.
- why_not_prior_failure: This avoids the exhausted CUTLASS tile/schedule/stage/caller family and does not retry request shaping, flat launch-capacity knobs, default prefix/chunked flags, fp8 KV bookkeeping, or the rejected ngram lookup windows. It is also distinct from prior kernel candidates: FlashInfer, Triton attention, FlashAttention 3, cuBLAS FP8 GEMM, reduce-overhead compile, and the deltanet kernel axis have already been measured or rejected.
