- speed_thesis: Set `request_shaping.target_concurrency: 2` to keep some decode batching benefit while reducing the KV-cache pressure, queueing, and scheduler contention seen as concurrency was raised to 4 and 8. This tests whether a smaller warm-window microbatch has better effective tok/s on the current vLLM runtime than the prior higher-concurrency points, without changing weights, kernels, prompts, or sampling.
- expected_affected_counter: Warm-window aggregate decode tok/s should be the primary affected counter. Secondary counters to inspect are running-request batch size, scheduler wait time, GPU KV-cache utilization, preemption/eviction events, and per-request tail latency across the four counted warm completions.
- quality_risk: Low semantic risk because this only changes request scheduling. Target weights, FP8 quantization, tokenizer, prompts, logits processors, temperature/top-p, seeds, and sampling policy remain unchanged. Residual risk is operational: concurrent scheduling can expose nondeterministic ordering, timeout, or truncation behavior if the runtime does not isolate per-request RNG and completion accounting.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not another CUTLASS tile, schedule, stage, or caller mutation; it uses the controller-supported serving surface. It also avoids repeating the prior failed `prefix_cache` schema and avoids duplicating the already-measured concurrency 4 and 8 settings, while still testing a serving-level lever that can change the available decode batching surface.
