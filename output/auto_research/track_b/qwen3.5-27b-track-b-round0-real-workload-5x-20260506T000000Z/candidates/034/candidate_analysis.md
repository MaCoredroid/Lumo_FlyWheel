- speed_thesis: Switch the repo-owned DeltaNet serving kernel to `triton-chunked-delta-v2`. The workload is warm decode, and the strategy brief names DeltaNet projection/gated attention paths as part of the decode bottleneck. This candidate changes an actual vLLM/kernel launch surface that has not been measured in this round, aiming to reduce per-token DeltaNet recurrent-path overhead without changing model weights, sampling, request shaping, or flat capacity knobs.
- expected_affected_counter: Warm real-workload decode tokens/sec should improve if the alternate DeltaNet kernel reduces decode-step latency; prefill and cold-start counters may be mostly unchanged. B-1/B-2 quality counters should remain equivalent because this is a kernel-selection change rather than a speculative-output or sampling change.
- quality_risk: Low to medium. Kernel-selection changes should preserve semantics, but an alternate Triton DeltaNet implementation can expose numerical or masking differences. The risk is lower than aggressive ngram speculative decoding because it does not draft, truncate, or accept generated text outside the target model path.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not a CUTLASS tile, schedule, stage, or caller mutation, so it avoids the prior bandwidth-bound CUTLASS negative memory. It is not request-shaping-only and not another flat `max_num_seqs`, `max_num_batched_tokens`, or `gpu_memory_utilization` launch-capacity attempt. It also avoids the exhausted ngram speculative families and directly follows the controller guidance to try an unmeasured `kernel_selection` surface before spending more candidates on speculative-depth search.
