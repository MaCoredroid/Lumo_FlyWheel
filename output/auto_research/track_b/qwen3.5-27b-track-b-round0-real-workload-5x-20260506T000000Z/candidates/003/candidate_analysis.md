- speed_thesis: Raise the controller-visible serving concurrency to 8 so the warm four-completion window can expose more decode batching and scheduler overlap. This does not make the FP8 GEMMs individually faster, but it may improve aggregate warm decode tokens/sec by amortizing launch/scheduler overhead and keeping the GPU fed across simultaneous requests.
- expected_affected_counter: Real workload warm-window aggregate decode throughput; secondary effects may appear in per-request latency and queueing counters if the gate records them. The candidate should not affect quality counters except through any timeout or truncation side effects from higher concurrency pressure.
- quality_risk: Low semantic risk because model weights, tokenizer, prompts, sampling parameters, and kernel math are unchanged. Operational risk is moderate: concurrency 8 can increase KV/cache pressure and tail latency, so failures would likely be OOM, timeout, or degraded counted throughput rather than distributional quality drift.
- why_not_prior_failure: Prior negative memory is specific to CUTLASS tile/schedule/stage/caller mutations that left per-token B-weight bytes unchanged and only moved speed by about 0.18-0.24%. This candidate changes the serving surface available to the benchmark by increasing concurrent decode batching instead of proposing another exhausted kernel schedule mutation.
