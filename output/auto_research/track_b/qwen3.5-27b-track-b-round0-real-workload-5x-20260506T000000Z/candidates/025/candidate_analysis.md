Secondary counters likely affected are speculative proposal count, accepted draft token count, and per-request warm completion latency.
- quality_risk: Lower than candidate 020 because each speculative step can advance at most 2 draft tokens instead of 3, reducing the chance of empty or prematurely truncated concurrent outputs. Residual risk remains from ngram speculative decoding under concurrent serving, especially if the broader lookup range finds stale prompt matches that the verifier must reject.
