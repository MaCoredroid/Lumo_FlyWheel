- speed_thesis: Use a narrow vLLM launch-capacity surface matched to the measured five-completion window: `max_num_seqs=5`, `max_num_batched_tokens=2048`, and `gpu_memory_utilization=0.88`. The thesis is that the default runtime may reserve and schedule for a much broader batch shape than this warm decode gate needs, adding scheduler/KV bookkeeping overhead without increasing useful decode parallelism. A five-sequence cap still lets the controller's per-task completion set co-reside while avoiding the larger sequence caps that previously failed to produce a measured win.
- expected_affected_counter: Warm decode tok/s should improve only if scheduler step latency, active sequence packing, KV-cache reservation pressure, or CUDA graph shape selection are currently paying overhead from oversized defaults. FP8 GEMM kernel timing is not expected to change directly; this candidate targets serving overhead around those kernels.
- quality_risk: Low. The candidate does not change model weights, prompts, tokenizer behavior, sampling parameters, quantization format, or decoding semantics. Operational risk is moderate: if the hidden workload needs more than five simultaneously active sequences, queueing could reduce throughput; if 2048 batched tokens is below an unexpectedly long prefill shape, startup/prefill behavior could be worse even though warm decode semantics remain unchanged.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not another CUTLASS tile, schedule, stage, or caller mutation, and it avoids request-shaping-only surfaces that are already exhausted. It also avoids the unsupported prefix-cache schema from `002`, the invalid `fp8_e4m3` KV dtype from `008`, the broad prefix/chunked/KV bundle from `009`, and the exact `max_num_seqs=4`/`max_num_batched_tokens=2048` surface from `010` whose controller failure was tied to a proxy port conflict rather than a measured speed result.
