- speed_thesis: Use vLLM ngram speculative decoding with a moderate speculative depth and a stricter prompt-lookup floor. The broader `prompt_lookup_max: 16` can still expose repeated prompt/completion spans in the warm 5-completion workload, while `num_speculative_tokens: 3` preserves the only local ngram depth that has shown a material warm-decode lift.
- expected_affected_counter: Warm counted decode tokens/sec should improve if the workload has repeated local ngrams long enough to accept drafted tokens. The main affected counter is decode throughput in the real vLLM warm window; prefill and cold completion behavior are secondary because the first cold completion is discarded.
- quality_risk: Medium. Ngram speculative decode is intended to preserve target-model acceptance semantics, but prior `num_speculative_tokens: 3, prompt_lookup_min: 2, prompt_lookup_max: 8` failed B-1 with empty or truncated concurrent outputs. Raising `prompt_lookup_min` to 4 reduces short-match false positives and should lower truncation risk, at the cost of fewer draft opportunities.
- why_not_prior_failure: This is not another CUTLASS tile/schedule/stage mutation and does not touch model weights or sampling. It also avoids the exhausted `num_speculative_tokens: 3, prompt_lookup_min: 2` family where max-only variants were already tested at 4, 6, and 8. Compared with the failed broad `min: 1, max: 16` run, this candidate keeps the broader lookup window but requires longer prompt matches before drafting.
