# Candidate 003 Analysis

- speed_thesis: Set request shaping to target concurrency 6, a midpoint between the audited concurrency-4 pass and the concurrency-8 full-gate divergence. This should still form warm decode batches large enough to amortize FP8 weight reads and launch overhead above the 15 tok/s target while reducing request interleaving pressure relative to candidate 002.
- expected_affected_counter: Aggregate warm decode tok/s should remain well above the serial baseline, with average active sequences per decode step and scheduled decode tokens per iteration between the candidate 001 and 002 profiles. If prefix-cache reuse is intact, cache hit/query counters should remain comparable to earlier Track B runs.
- quality_risk: Model quality risk is low because weights, tokenizer, logits, and sampling behavior are unchanged. The main risk is scheduler-induced divergence from different batching boundaries; concurrency 6 is intended to lower the B-3 serial-vs-batched divergence risk seen at concurrency 8 while preserving most of the batching benefit.
- why_not_prior_failure: This does not revisit the exhausted CUTLASS tile/schedule/stage/caller surface that repeatedly produced sub-0.25% lifts. It changes the serving surface by shaping concurrent cache-hit requests, so the tested lever is batch formation and runtime utilization rather than another bandwidth-bound kernel mutation.
