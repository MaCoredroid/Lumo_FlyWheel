It imports `lumo_flywheel_serving.fr13_fixed32_commit_slot_scatter` at tree line 25075 and `fr13_host_tail_prep` at 25105 and 22990; the latter bakes tree-depth literals. Later full-launcher Arctic installation/import (8164 onward), GPU preparation, live verifier and serving steps are outside this proposed source-only execution; do not run the surrounding shell body to obtain hashes.
