A joint source sealer must join those two observations from the **same request/owner/generation/prefix/root**, prove no intervening MTP or target mutation, and bind their raw payloads together. This avoids inventing a new MTP recurrence or reimplementing its input shift.

The archived target-only source lacks the prefix target hidden/MTP history required to reconstruct this pairing. Generate and freeze a new paired source inventory; do not silently replace the existing source manifest. ## 4. What this can qualify, and the minimum remaining gates

A valid joint source and exact import are structural evidence. Byte-exact candidate remap and untouched-region checks are structural publication evidence. Equality of common O0 does not imply equality of subsequently computed native/candidate MTP K/V or scores. Preserve raw state/score differences; do not apply an invented BF16 tolerance or declare numerical equivalence from a boolean remap receipt. Existing Q1 protocol makes top-k categorical and the operational target contract lists drafter top-k as diagnostics; a dedicated MTP acceptance criterion must be explicitly frozen before interpreting candidate MTP results as a pass. Reuse any subsequently approved criterion verbatim, with native repeat eligibility and unchanged denominators; the target greedy rule alone is not an MTP criterion.

Minimal implementation sequence is: joint source sealer/manifest; native+candidate joint import wrapper and imported-prefix audit; actual candidate MTP score/boundary observation; immutable source/job/driver/reducer wiring. Focused CPU controls need wrong source-root/history join, wrong singleton/group/block units, missing/short objects, overlap/identity replacement, partial-write fail-stop, changed import prefix, wrong shifted token/hidden/position, and exact publication source-map/after-seal joins. Existing controls need not be rerun wholesale.

Then reuse the already declared calibration/native-first scheduling and source/model/settings checks. Joint native A/B/R2 repeatability must be observed with MTP actually loaded and the same joint source; the running target-only common-O0 results cannot establish it because they have no MTP allocation/history. Candidate observations follow reference eligibility under a separate gate. Three source boundaries are not three qualified path cases; all84 paths/prefix combinations remain required, and no unstable case may be dropped. Continuous cycles/APC/lifecycle remain their existing separate requirements; hydrate only at their declared initial boundary, never between cycles.
