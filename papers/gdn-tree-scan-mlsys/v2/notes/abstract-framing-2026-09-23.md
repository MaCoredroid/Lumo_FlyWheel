# Agent behavior and comparative speed framing

Author steering: abstract should lead with real agent execution, observed decoding stability, and speed relative to competing mechanisms. Audit chronology, exact request count, resumed-run accounting, individual outcome counts and failed compact-candidate gates remain in the evaluation/artifact rather than dominating the abstract.

Current evidence supports one selected ten-task deployment with continued tool use and nonempty patches, and a defined workload decode statistic. A claim of no detected degeneration must name the finite trace-check scope, retain the earlier campaign counterexample in the body, and must not be elevated to unchanged task quality or universal degeneration freedom.

The desired missing headline is a matched native-versus-tree speed result on full tasks, accompanied by behavior/outcome checks. Existing targeted native probes use different serving settings and give no full-cohort baseline. Local Bole/TreeWY-family arithmetic studies are not author-system speed comparisons and failed their local promotion criteria. No comparative speed number is invented or imported from a different workload.
