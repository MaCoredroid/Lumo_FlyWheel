The coding-agent study completes 30 attempts, one per task and arm (Table~\ref{tab:agent-study}; task-level outcomes in Appendix~\ref{app:workload}). \sys{} resolves five tasks with eight nonempty patches, of which three fail tests, and two empty patches; native MTP-5 resolves six with two failing and two empty; SGLang resolves five with three failing and two empty. Empty patches count as failures without invoking the evaluator. The live trajectories diverge after the first tool call: \sys{} emits 258,889 output tokens over 248 requests, against 134,643 over 224 for MTP-5 and 158,827 over 210 for SGLang, so its lower pooled decode rate and longer summed agent duration here contrast with the fixed-request replays. The replay check in Section~\ref{sec:res-sampler} finds no large aggregate length shift between the arms on fixed prompts; the cause of the live token-volume difference remains unresolved. The study is descriptive: one attempt per arm, ten selected tasks, no plain-decoding agent control, and no benchmark-wide quality estimate.
