# FR14 — RadixArk aggressive NVFP4 arm: hardware floor, re-derived from the on-disk ledger

Generated by `radixark_floor_derivation.py` from `radixark_ledger.json`, which is
built by reading the **safetensors headers only** of
`/models/qwen3.8-27b-nvfp4-radixark` @ `554ebba9b5f1b79dc11246341960360e6ef05ef4`.

**No constant was scaled.** The staged design note's estimate was not trusted; every
term below is a sum of real tensor spans. The ledger's own total is
`21,921,428,072` B across `2194` tensors, which equals the repo's
`qualification.json:checkpoint.output_indexed_payload_bytes` exactly — the ledger and
the publisher's audit agree byte-for-byte.

Bandwidth: **273,000,000,000 B/s** (the FR13 measured GB10 unified-memory figure, unchanged).

    floor_ms = mandatory_weight_bytes × 1000 / 273,000,000,000

Reading that table: `U8` is the packed NVFP4 weight payload — 192 layers here, 193
counting the head, matching `tensor-audit.json`'s `NVFP4: 193`. The 400 `F8_E4M3`
tensors split into 208 FP8 weights (== `tensor-audit.json`'s `FP8: 208`) and 192 NVFP4
per-16 block scales. The 800 `F32` tensors are all 0-dim scalar global scales — 3.2 kB
in total, i.e. noise — and with the head's 2 they make 802 = 401 `input_scale` + 193
`weight_scale_2` + 208 FP8 per-tensor `weight_scale`. `BF16` is what the recipe left
unconverted (GDN `in_proj_a`/`in_proj_b`, conv, norms).

That is **0.7152 GB against 2.5428 GB** for the BF16 head the
conservative arm ended up serving — a **1.8276 GB** saving on the
verifier head alone, worth **6.695 ms** of floor.

The `fp8_head` figure is not a guess: the unsloth lm_head surgery's own manifest
records the shard growing by `1,270,901,784` B when it replaced the FP8 head with the
BF16 one, so that FP8 head was `2,542,796,800 - 1,270,901,784 = 1,271,895,016` B on
disk. The computed `1,271,895,040` differs by 24 B — safetensors header text, not payload.

### The DVK slice is row-aligned by construction

The NVFP4 block scales are `[out, in/16]` = `[248320, 320]`, so a 65536-row
`index_select` selects consistent rows of the weight **and** the scale. The K64 draft
head therefore slices out of the NVFP4 head with no repacking:
`65536×2560 + 65536×320 = 188,743,680` B versus `671,088,640` B for the BF16 read.

## 4. Where the ceiling lands

The conservative unsloth arm is pinned at **102.480 ms** (`floor_ledger.json`,
`root_64k_five_64k_draft_heads`). Against that:

* **Phase 1, shippable the moment the lm_head patch boots: 92.345 ms** (−10.135 ms, −9.9%).
* Phase 2 (FP4 draft-head GEMV): **83.511 ms** (−18.969 ms). This is the low-80s band the directive asked for.
* Phase 2 + MTP-FP8 requant: **75.735 ms**.
* Plan B (FP8 head instead of NVFP4): **94.384 ms** — 2.039 ms worse than Phase 1, i.e. the NVFP4 head is worth 2.039 ms over the FP8 fallback (0.557 GB).
* Worst case (BF16 head, as the unsloth arm was forced into): **99.040 ms** — only 3.440 ms better than the conservative arm. **The aggressive backbone alone buys almost nothing; nearly all of the win is the head.** That is why the lm_head loader patch is the whole ballgame for this arm.

## 5. What is NOT in these numbers

* The honest-floor per-request geometry term (**+7.117 ms at C=18k**) is unchanged and
  additive; it is geometry-only and carries over from FR13 untouched.
* KV-cache traffic, activations, and every non-weight cost: `nonweight_costs_included`
  is `false` in every scenario, as in the unsloth ledger.
* `embed_tokens` (2.543 GB) and the vision tower (0.921 GB) are excluded — neither is
  read on a decode step.
* Acceptance. Floor ms is per *step*; TPS also needs the measured acceptance length.
  RadixArk's own SGLang qualification measured MTP acceptance **2.775** (chain EAGLE
  3/1/4, GB300 ×4) — a prior, not a gate, for Hydra27's tree.
